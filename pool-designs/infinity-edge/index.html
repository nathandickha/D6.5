Infinity Edge

Spillover edge overlooking the valley

Designed for sites with a view. The Infinity Edge draws the water toward the horizon, spilling over a single far edge into a catch basin. Brushed basalt paving and polished concrete coping keep the palette monolithic, letting the water and the landscape do the talking.
